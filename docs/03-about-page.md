# 03 — About Page

**Route:** `/about`
**File:** `app/about/page.tsx`
**Status:** planned

## Goal

Build trust. Tell drivers and travelers who runs this place and why it's a reliable stop.

## Sections

1. **Page hero** — H1 "About Blue Mountain Travel Center" + sub-headline
2. **Our story** — One or two paragraphs about origin, ownership, years in operation
3. **Our mission** — Brief mission statement (cleanliness, safety, hospitality)
4. **Why drivers trust us** — 3–4 small cards (e.g., family-owned, on-duty staff 24/7, well-lit lot)
5. **Photo strip / facility tour** — 3–4 photos of the lot, restaurant, showers, store
6. **Contact CTA** — Quick call/get-directions block

## Data dependencies

- `lib/site-config.ts` — business name and contact

## Content needed from owner

- Founding year / how long in operation
- Owner / family name (optional, but humanizes the page)
- Mission statement (we can draft and the owner edits)
- 3–4 facility photos
